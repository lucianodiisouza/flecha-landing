Flecha's sidebar has three kinds of tabs. Where a tab lives decides what closing it does.

| | Where | Closing it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Favorites** | The icons at the top, the same in every Space | Unloads it and sends it back to its saved address |
| **Pinned tabs** | Under the favorites, per Space, with folders | Unloads it and sends it back to its pinned address |
| **Today** | The list below, per Space | Archives it |

The close button says **Unload tab** on favorites and pinned tabs, and **Close tab** on Today tabs. Middle-click also closes.

## Move tabs around

Drag a tab:

- to the favorites bar to make it a favorite,
- into the pinned list, or onto a folder, to pin it,
- into Today, or onto **New Tab**, to unpin it,
- onto the page to open it in [split view](/docs/guides/peek-split-flechinha/#split-view).

When a tab becomes pinned or a favorite, its current address becomes the one it returns to. If you've wandered off, right-click it and choose **Go Back to Pinned URL**.

## Folders

- Create one with **New Folder** in the Space's right-click menu. It's added at the end of the pinned list.
- Double-click a folder's name to rename it. Click it to open or close it.
- Drag tabs onto it to file them.
- To delete a folder, use Settings › **Tabs & Spaces**. Flecha asks whether to keep its tabs or delete them too.

## Today tabs and the archive

Every tab you open from the [command bar](/docs/command-bar/) starts in Today. Today tabs archive themselves when you haven't used them for a while:

- 12 hours by default; change it in Settings › **Tabs** › **Archive Today tabs after** to 1 day, 7 days, 30 days or Never,
- the tab you're looking at is never archived,
- **Clear** next to the Today heading, or <kbd>⇧</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>K</kbd>, archives all of this Space's Today tabs now.

The archive keeps the last 500 tabs. Find them by typing in the command bar: they show up under **Archive**. <kbd>⇧</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>T</kbd> reopens the newest one.

## The tab menu

Right-click a tab for: **Open in Split View**, **Copy Link**, **Copy as Markdown Link**, **Rename…**, **Duplicate**, **Open in Flechinha**, **Pin Tab**, **Add to Favorites**, **Move to Space**, **Reload**, **Mute Tab**, **Go Back to Pinned URL** and **Close Tab**. Today tabs also have **Close Tabs Below** and **Close Other Tabs**.

Shortcuts for all of these are in [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/shortcuts/#tabs).
