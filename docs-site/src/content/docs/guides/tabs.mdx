Flecha's sidebar has three kinds of tabs. Where a tab lives decides what closing it does.

| | Where | Closing it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Favorites** | The icons at the top, the same in every Space | Unloads it and sends it back to its saved address |
| **Pinned tabs** | Under the favorites, per Space, with folders | Unloads it and sends it back to its pinned address |
| **Today** | The list below, per Space | Archives it |

The close button says **Unload tab** on favorites and pinned tabs, and **Close tab** on Today tabs. Middle-click also closes.

## Move tabs around

Drag a tab:

- to the favorites bar to make it a favorite,
- into the pinned list, or onto a folder, to pin it,
- into Today, or onto **New Tab**, to unpin it,
- onto the page to open it in [split view](/docs/guides/peek-split-flechinha/#split-view).

Pinned tabs reorder by dragging: drop on the top or bottom of a row to move the tab there, or on the middle of the row to open the two in split view. Favorites reorder by dragging too and fill their row; the active one has a ring in the Space's colour.

When a tab becomes pinned or a favorite, its current address becomes the one it returns to. If you've wandered off, right-click it and choose **Go Back to Pinned URL**.

## Select several tabs

<kbd>⌘</kbd>-click or <kbd>⇧</kbd>-click tabs in the sidebar to select more than one. With several selected:

- <kbd>⇧</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>C</kbd> copies all their links, one per line,
- <kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>C</kbd> copies them as a Markdown list,
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> clears the selection.

## Folders

- Create one with **New Folder** on the Pinned header, in the Space's right-click menu or in the **Tabs** menu. It's added at the end of the pinned list.
- Right-click a tab and choose **Move to New Folder**, or **Move to Folder** to file it in one you already have.
- Double-click a folder's name to rename it. Click it to open or close it.
- Drag tabs onto it to file them. Drag a folder to move it, or drop it on another folder to nest it.
- Right-click a folder for its own menu.
- Folders and pinned tabs can have an emoji as their icon: right-click and choose **Change Icon…**, or **Remove Icon** to go back.
- To delete a folder, use Settings › **Tabs & Spaces**. Flecha asks whether to keep its tabs or delete them too.

## Today tabs and the archive

Every tab you open from the [command bar](/docs/command-bar/) starts in Today. Today tabs archive themselves when you haven't used them for a while:

- 12 hours by default; change it in Settings › **Tabs** › **Archive Today tabs after** to 1 day, 7 days, 30 days or Never,
- the tab you're looking at is never archived,
- **Clear** next to the Today heading, or <kbd>⇧</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>K</kbd>, archives all of this Space's Today tabs now.

The archive keeps the last 500 tabs. Find them by typing in the command bar: they show up under **Archive**. <kbd>⇧</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>T</kbd> reopens the newest one.

Settings › **Archive** lists the whole archive: search it, reopen a tab, forget one, or clear it. The **Archive** menu in the menu bar has the last 10 archived tabs, and **View Archive** opens the full list.

## The tab menu

Right-click a tab for: **Open in Split View**, **Copy Link**, **Copy as Markdown Link**, **Rename…**, **Duplicate**, **Open in Flechinha**, **Pin Tab**, **Add to Favorites**, **Move to Space**, **Move to New Folder**, **Move to Folder**, **Reload**, **Mute Tab**, **Go Back to Pinned URL** and **Close Tab**. Today tabs also have **Close Tabs Below** and **Close Other Tabs**. Pinned tabs have **Change Icon…**, and a tab in split view has **Swap Sides** and **Exit Split View**.

Shortcuts for all of these are in [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/shortcuts/#tabs).

## The Tabs and Archive menus

Flecha has its own menus in the Mac menu bar. **Tabs** has pin and unpin, **New Folder**, **Reveal Tab in Sidebar**, **Copy Link**, **Copy as Markdown Link**, **Copy Selection as Quote**, **Duplicate**, **Mute Tab**, split view (split with a new tab, **Swap Sides**, **Exit Split View**) and **Clear Today**. **Archive** lists the last 10 archived tabs to reopen, and **View Archive**. The **Spaces** menu is described in [Spaces](/docs/guides/spaces/#the-spaces-menu-in-the-menu-bar).

- **Reveal Tab in Sidebar** shows where the current tab is in the sidebar.
- **Copy Selection as Quote** copies the text you've selected on the page as a Markdown quote with a link back to the page.
- **Capture Full Page**, in the [command bar](/docs/command-bar/) (<kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>T</kbd>, type `capture`), saves a PNG of the whole page, not only what's on screen, to your Downloads folder.

## Video and audio

- When a tab is playing, a card above the sidebar footer shows it. Click it to go to that tab, pause or play from it, or dismiss it.
- A video that's playing pops into a floating picture-in-picture player when you switch to another tab or Space, on any site. Go back to its tab and it returns to the page.

## Stay On Top

**Stay On Top**, in the **Window** menu or the [command bar](/docs/command-bar/), keeps the Flecha window above other apps. Settings floats along with it.
