A Space is a set of pinned tabs, folders and Today tabs with its own name, icon and colour. Use one for work, one for a side project, one per client. Favorites are shared: they stay in every Space.

## Create a Space

Any of these creates a Space and opens its editor:

- the **+** button at the bottom of the sidebar,
- <kbd>⌃</kbd><kbd>⇧</kbd><kbd>N</kbd>,
- **New Space** in the [command bar](/docs/command-bar/),
- **New Space** in Settings › **Tabs & Spaces**.

In the editor, give it a name, pick an **Icon** (an emoji) and a **Colour**, or a custom one. In dark mode the colour becomes a deep tint of the same hue.

To edit a Space later, double-click its name at the top of the sidebar, or right-click it and choose **Edit Space…**.

## Switch Spaces

- <kbd>⌃</kbd><kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>⌃</kbd><kbd>9</kbd> jumps to a Space.
- <kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>→</kbd> and <kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>←</kbd> go to the next and previous, wrapping around.
- Swipe sideways with two fingers over the sidebar to move one Space at a time.
- Click a Space's dot at the bottom of the sidebar.

## Separate cookies and logins

Turn this on for a Space and it gets its own cookies, local storage, cache and service workers. You can be signed in to GitHub as two people, or test a site as an admin in one Space and as a customer in another, side by side.

- In the Space editor, turn on **Separate cookies and logins**, or right-click the Space and choose **Separate Cookies and Logins**.
- A lock next to the Space's name shows it's on.
- Turning it on or off reopens the Space's tabs so they move to the right storage.
- **Clear Cookies and Site Data…** in the Space's right-click menu signs you out of everything in that Space only.

<Aside>
Favorites always use the shared storage, even when you open them from a Space with separate cookies. Keep the sites you need to sign in to separately as pinned tabs in that Space.
</Aside>

## The Space menu

Right-click a Space's name, its dot, or an empty part of the sidebar:

- **Edit Space…**
- **New Folder**
- **Separate Cookies and Logins**
- **Clear Cookies and Site Data…** (when cookies are separate)
- **Delete Space…**

Deleting a Space closes its pinned and Today tabs for good; they don't go to the archive. Flecha asks first. The **Delete Space** button inside the editor deletes right away.

## Organise everything at once

Settings › **Tabs & Spaces** shows every Space, its pinned tabs, folders and Today tabs in one list. Drag tabs and folders between Spaces, select several with <kbd>⌘</kbd>-click or <kbd>⇧</kbd>-click, reorder Spaces by dragging their headers, and search with <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>F</kbd>.
