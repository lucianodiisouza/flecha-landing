The Browser Company is [sunsetting Arc](https://arc.net/): **Arc stops working on macOS and Windows on December 22, 2026**, and Arc accounts and their data are deleted within 30 days after that. Flecha keeps the ideas that made Arc worth using: a vertical sidebar, Spaces, pinned tabs that come back to where they started, Peek and split view. It is real Chromium, so your Chrome extensions work and you can sign in to Google sites as usual, and it's open source, so it can't be shut down from one day to the next.

New to the shutdown? [Arc is shutting down: what to do before December 22](/docs/arc-shutdown/) has the dates and a checklist of what to save. Comparing options? See [the best Arc alternatives in 2026](/docs/compare/arc-alternatives/).

Flecha's interface is rebuilt from scratch. It uses no Arc code and is not affiliated with The Browser Company.

## Before you start

- **Keep Arc installed** until you've imported and checked your Spaces. Flecha reads Arc's data folder on your Mac, not your Arc account.
- **Save what Flecha doesn't import:** passwords, extensions, Boosts' code, Easels and Notes. The [checklist](/docs/arc-shutdown/#your-checklist-before-december-22) covers each one.
- **Check streaming:** Flecha has no Widevine DRM, so Netflix, Disney+ and Spotify's web player [open in Safari instead](/docs/guides/privacy/#streaming-services-and-drm).

## Import your Arc sidebar

<Steps>

1. [Install Flecha](/docs/install/), and **before opening it for the first time**, [set up the helper](/docs/install/#set-up-the-helper-for-importing-and-coding-agents). Importing reads Arc's files on your Mac, which needs the helper.

2. Open Flecha. The welcome tour starts on its own.

3. On the **Bring your stuff over** step, click **Import** next to Arc. Flecha shows how many bookmarks and pages it brought in.

</Steps>

If you've already been through the tour, import from Settings › **Import** instead. When macOS asks to let Flecha use Arc's item in the Keychain, that's how Flecha reads your Arc cookies so sites stay signed in; the tour explains it too.

### What comes over from Arc

Flecha reads your sidebar from `~/Library/Application Support/Arc/StorableSidebar.json` and your history from Arc's profile folders. It only reads them; nothing is uploaded or changed.

| In Arc | In Flecha |
| --- | --- |
| Spaces, with their name, icon and colour | Spaces with the same name, icon and colour |
| Spaces in your other Arc profiles | Spaces with their own cookies and logins, so each profile's accounts stay apart |
| Pinned tabs and folders, including nested folders | Pinned tabs and folders in the same Space |
| Unpinned tabs | Today tabs (folders are flattened) |
| Favorites (the icons at the top of the sidebar) | Favorites |
| History | History, searchable from the command bar |

These stay behind: archived tabs, Little Arc windows, Easels, Arc Boosts, Space themes and passwords.

<Aside>
Imported history keeps the pages but not their original visit times or titles. They count as visited on the day you import.
</Aside>

## If something doesn't come over

**Arc isn't in the list, or the import does nothing.** The helper isn't registered. Quit Flecha, run the [helper script](/docs/install/#set-up-the-helper-for-importing-and-coding-agents) (again with the new path if you moved Flecha.app), then import from Settings › **Import**.

**macOS asks about "Arc Safe Storage".** That's the Keychain item where Arc keeps the key to its cookies. Choose **Allow** and your sites stay signed in. If you chose **Deny**, your Spaces and tabs still imported, but you'll be signed out; import Arc again and choose **Allow** to bring the cookies over.

**A Space is missing.** If you used several Arc profiles, their Spaces arrive as separate Spaces with their own logins; look for them in the Space dots at the bottom of the sidebar or in the **Spaces** menu.

**My unpinned folders are flat.** Unpinned tabs come in as Today tabs, and Today tabs don't have folders. Drag the ones you want to keep into the pinned list or onto a folder, or right-click one and choose **Move to New Folder**.

**My extensions are gone.** Extensions aren't imported. Install them again from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/); they work as in Chrome.

**My passwords aren't there.** Flecha never imports passwords. Use a password manager extension or app, and move your logins into it while Arc still opens.

**History shows the wrong dates.** Imported history keeps the pages but not their visit times, so everything counts as visited on the day you import.

Still stuck? [Open an issue](https://github.com/lucianodiisouza/flecha-browser/issues) with what you see.

## Your first day in Flecha

<Steps>

1. **Make Flecha your default browser** in Settings › **General** › **Default browser**, so links from other apps stop opening in Arc. They'll open in a [Flechinha](/docs/guides/peek-split-flechinha/#flechinha).

2. **Rebuild your Air Traffic Control rules** in Settings › **Tabs**: a site, or a site and a path, and the Space it should open in. [How it works](/docs/guides/spaces/#send-links-from-other-apps-to-a-space)

3. **Choose how long Today tabs live.** 12 hours by default; change it to 1 day, 7 days, 30 days or never in Settings › **Tabs**. Archived tabs are in the command bar and Settings › **Archive**.

4. **Decide which Spaces should keep logins apart.** Imported Spaces share cookies so your sign-ins keep working. Turn on **Separate cookies and logins** in a Space's editor for work and personal accounts side by side. [Spaces](/docs/guides/spaces/#separate-cookies-and-logins)

5. **Try the command bar.** <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>T</kbd> searches tabs, history and the archive. If you write code, try `gh`, `npm` or `mdn`, or paste a JWT. [Command bar](/docs/command-bar/)

6. **Bring your Boosts back.** Open the site, press <kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>B</kbd> and paste your CSS or script. [Boosts](/docs/guides/boosts/)

7. **Reinstall your extensions**, starting with your password manager.

</Steps>

Ad and tracker blocking is already on, picture-in-picture pops out on its own when you switch tabs, and **Stay On Top** in the **Window** menu keeps Flecha above other apps.

## Importing from other browsers

The same **Bring your stuff over** step also reads bookmarks and history from Chrome, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi, Opera, Opera GX, Chromium, Firefox and Safari.

- Bookmarks become pinned tabs in a new Space named after the browser, with one folder per profile.
- Up to 2,000 bookmarks and 5,000 history entries per profile come over. Only `http` and `https` pages are imported.
- Safari's data can only be read with Full Disk Access. If Flecha asks for it, turn it on in System Settings › Privacy & Security › **Full Disk Access**, then import again.
- Passwords are never imported. Cookies come over from Chromium browsers and Firefox, so sites stay signed in; Safari's don't yet.
