The Browser Company is [sunsetting Arc](https://arc.net/). New accounts closed on October 22, 2026, and **Arc stops working on macOS and Windows on December 22, 2026**. Your Arc account and its data are deleted within 30 days after that. If you still use Arc every day, now is the time to choose where to go.

<Aside type="caution" title="Arc stops on December 22, 2026">
Anything you haven't moved out of Arc by then stays behind. [What to do before Arc shuts down](/docs/arc-shutdown/) has the full timeline and a checklist.
</Aside>

This page compares the browsers Arc users most often move to. We make Flecha, so we've linked the source for every claim about other browsers and kept the comparison to things you can check.

## Flecha

[Flecha](https://flecha.dev/) is an open-source Chromium browser for Mac, built by a developer who didn't want to give up Arc. It keeps Arc's vertical sidebar, Spaces, pinned tabs that return to where they started, Today tabs that archive themselves, Peek, split view and a Little Arc–style window called Flechinha. It [imports your Arc sidebar](/docs/switch-from-arc/), Spaces and folders included.

It adds tools for people who build websites: a [command bar](/docs/command-bar/) that understands `gh`, `npm` and `mdn` and decodes JWTs, one key to move a page between local, staging and production, your dev servers in the sidebar, and a [built-in MCP server](/docs/agents/mcp/) so Claude Code or Cursor can read and drive your real tabs.

**Good fit if** you're on a Mac, you liked Arc's interface more than its AI features, and you want a browser that can't be shut down: the code is MIT-licensed and public.

**Not a fit if** you need Windows, Linux, a phone app or sync between computers. Flecha has no account, so there's no sync. It also doesn't include Widevine, so Netflix, Disney+, Spotify's web player and other DRM streaming [open in Safari instead](/docs/guides/privacy/#streaming-services-and-drm).

## Dia

[Dia](https://www.diabrowser.com/) is The Browser Company's AI browser, and where it points Arc users. It's Chromium-based, runs on macOS and Windows, and imports Arc's Spaces as Profiles, folders as tab groups, plus passwords, history and extensions ([arc.net](https://arc.net/)). The core browser is free; AI features are on paid plans ([pricing](https://www.diabrowser.com/pricing)). Arc's sidebar becomes an optional layout, and Boosts and Easels have no equivalent.

**Good fit if** you want an AI assistant built into the browser and you're comfortable with an account and a closed-source app. [Flecha vs Dia](/docs/compare/flecha-vs-dia/)

## Zen

[Zen](https://zen-browser.app/) is an open-source browser built on Firefox, for macOS, Windows and Linux. It has workspaces, a compact mode, split view and Glance, its version of Peek ([docs](https://docs.zen-browser.app/)). Since version 1.24b it imports Spaces and Profiles from Arc and Dia ([release notes](https://zen-browser.app/release-notes)). Because it's Firefox, Chrome Web Store extensions don't work, and it doesn't play Netflix or other Widevine-protected streaming yet ([FAQ](https://docs.zen-browser.app/faq)).

**Good fit if** you want an Arc-like layout on Linux or prefer Firefox's engine. [Flecha vs Zen](/docs/compare/flecha-vs-zen/)

## Vivaldi

[Vivaldi](https://vivaldi.com/) is a highly configurable Chromium browser with vertical tabs, workspaces, tab tiling, built-in mail and an ad and tracker blocker, on desktop and mobile. It deliberately doesn't add generative AI ([Vivaldi 7.8](https://vivaldi.com/blog/vivaldi-7-8-launches-with-message-to-big-tech-humans-dont-need-ai-babysitters/)). We couldn't find an Arc importer.

**Good fit if** you like to configure everything and want the same browser on every OS. [Flecha vs Vivaldi](/docs/compare/flecha-vs-vivaldi/)

## Brave

[Brave](https://brave.com/) is an open-source Chromium browser that blocks ads and trackers by default, with optional vertical tabs, split view and the Leo AI assistant, on desktop and mobile. Widevine is off until you turn it on ([help](https://support.brave.app/hc/en-us/articles/360023851591)).

**Good fit if** you want strong blocking defaults on every device and don't need Arc's Spaces. [Flecha vs Brave](/docs/compare/flecha-vs-brave/)

## Microsoft Edge

[Edge](https://www.microsoft.com/edge) has vertical tabs, Workspaces and split screen, plus Copilot built in ([Microsoft](https://blogs.windows.com/msedgedev/2026/05/13/new-updates-to-edge-across-desktop-and-mobile/)). It needs macOS 13 or newer on a Mac ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/deployedge/microsoft-edge-supported-operating-systems)).

**Good fit if** you want a mainstream browser with vertical tabs, or your company already uses Microsoft 365. [Flecha vs Edge](/docs/compare/flecha-vs-edge/)

## Chrome

Chrome has had native vertical tabs since spring 2026 (right-click a tab › **Show Tabs Vertically**), but no Spaces, Peek or Little Arc. Usage statistics are sent when "Help improve Chrome's features and performance" is on ([Google](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/14746339)).

**Good fit if** you mostly wanted Arc for vertical tabs and are happy in Google's ecosystem. [Flecha vs Chrome](/docs/compare/flecha-vs-chrome/)

## Others

- **[Orion](https://orionbrowser.com/)** by Kagi: a WebKit browser for macOS and iOS with zero telemetry, vertical tabs and partial support for Chrome and Firefox extensions. It lists Arc among its import sources. Kagi ended Orion for Linux and Windows in October 2026. [Flecha vs Orion](/docs/compare/flecha-vs-orion/)
- **[Helium](https://helium.computer/)**: an open-source (GPL-3.0) Chromium browser without Google's services, with ad blocking, vertical tabs and split view, in beta for macOS, Windows and Linux. No Widevine and no sync yet. [Flecha vs Helium](/docs/compare/flecha-vs-helium/)
- **[Comet](https://www.perplexity.ai/comet)** by Perplexity: a free Chromium browser built around an AI assistant that automates tasks, on desktop and mobile.
- **ChatGPT Atlas** shut down on August 9, 2026, so it's no longer an option.

## Moving from Arc to Flecha

1. [Install Flecha](/docs/install/) for Apple silicon or Intel.
2. Set up the helper, then [import your Arc sidebar](/docs/switch-from-arc/) in the welcome tour.
3. Learn the [shortcuts](/docs/shortcuts/): most of Arc's are the same.

Do it before December 22, while Arc still opens, so you can check nothing is missing side by side.
